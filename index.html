Questions

Common questions

I already have a Facebook page. Why do I need a website?

Lots of customers search Google, not Facebook. A website shows up in search, looks more professional, and puts your hours, prices and phone number in one place.

How long does it take?

Your free preview is usually ready in a day or two. Once you say yes, the site can be live within a week.

What do I need to do?

Not much. Send me any photos you'd like on the site and check that your info is right. I handle the rest.

What if I want to cancel?

No long contracts. If you stop the monthly plan, the web address is still yours.
