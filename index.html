Questions

Common questions

I already have a Facebook page. Why do I need a website?

Many customers search Google, not Facebook. A website shows up in search, looks more professional, and puts your hours, prices and phone number in one place. Your site can link to your Facebook page too.

How long does it take?

Your free preview is usually ready in a day or two. Once you say yes, your site can be live within a week.

What do I need to do?

Not much. Send any photos you'd like on the site and check that your info is right. I handle the rest.

How much does it cost?

$300 one time to set up, then $25 a month for hosting and updates. Your web address is about $12 a year.

What if I want to cancel?

No long contracts. If you stop the monthly plan, your web address is still yours.

Can you update my site later?

Yes. Send me new hours, prices or photos and I'll update your site. Small changes are included in the monthly plan.
